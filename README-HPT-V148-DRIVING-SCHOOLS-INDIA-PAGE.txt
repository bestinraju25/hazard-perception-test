PURPOSE
A standalone page to share with motor driving schools across India. It explains what hazard perception is, examples of developing road hazards, how the web assessment works, the current platform format, school onboarding, branded access, learner workflow and school-side result visibility. No pricing information is included.

FORMAT / REGULATORY NOTE
The 10-clip, 50-second, 100-mark, 60-mark benchmark and five-response limit shown on the page describe the current HPT web platform configuration (as defined in hpt-config.js and the candidate instructions), not a uniform national statutory exam specification. The page explicitly states that it is an independent supplementary platform, not an official government or RTO examination service.

Public reporting on Kerala MVD’s announced format describes 10 one-minute clips and a 60-mark pass threshold, with the announced effective date of 1 December 2026. The new page links to Onmanorama and The New Indian Express and advises schools to check their relevant transport authority’s current directions.

VALIDATION
The new page is standalone and responsive, metadata/canonical/schema are included, no price or currency information appears on the new page, the homepage has links to it, and sitemap.xml includes its canonical URL. Archive integrity and local references are checked in the build notes.
