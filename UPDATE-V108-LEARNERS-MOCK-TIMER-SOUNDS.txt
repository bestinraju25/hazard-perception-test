HAZARD PERCEPTION TEST WEBSITE — V108 UPDATE
LEARNERS MOCK TEST TIMER, SOUND & COUNTDOWN FEEDBACK

Changes in this update
- Added a 30-second countdown for every Learners Mock Test question.
- When a question reaches zero, it is marked unanswered and the test automatically advances.
- The final question automatically submits when its 30 seconds expire.
- Kept the overall 15-minute (900-second) examination limit.
- Added a short, identical option-selection sound for every answer choice. The sound does not distinguish correct and incorrect answers.
- Added a quiet clock-like tick once per second during the question countdown.
- Added a visual warning in the final 10 seconds and a subtle countdown shake in the final 5 seconds.
- Kept the new sounds local and synthesized with the browser audio engine; no extra audio files or external audio libraries are required.

Scope
These changes are limited to the Learners Mock Test. The Hazard Perception Test and Learner Practice mode are not changed.
