KEYWORD STRATEGY
Do not place a huge comma-separated keyword list or repeat the same phrases unnaturally. Google does not use the meta keywords tag, and keyword stuffing can violate Search spam policies. Use a small number of closely related phrases naturally in each page title, H1, headings, body text, image alt text and descriptive internal links.

INTENT MISMATCH TO AVOID
Broad phrases such as “best driving school”, “driving school near me”, “driving licence”, “Kerala MVD” or “learner's licence” can mean that a searcher wants a driving-school directory, local driving lessons, official government services or licence application services. This HPT platform should not claim to provide those services. Target those broad phrases only with genuinely useful, accurate content that matches the visitor's intent.
