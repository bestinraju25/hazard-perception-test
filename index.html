A smarter approach to hazard perception and road safety education

The Hazard Perception Test in Kerala is a video-based learning and assessment experience that helps learners recognise developing road hazards before they become dangerous situations. Students watch realistic driving scenarios and respond when they identify a developing hazard. This practical format encourages observation, anticipation and timely decision-making—skills that support safer driving on busy roads, junctions and changing traffic conditions.

Why driving schools should use a school road safety test

Driving schools play an important role in preparing responsible road users, not just helping candidates learn vehicle controls. A structured School Road Safety Test in India gives instructors an additional way to discuss hazard awareness, vulnerable road users, safe following distances and emerging traffic risks. Video-based practice can make these lessons more engaging, while examination results help students understand where further learning may be useful.

Interactive hazard perception training for students and schools

Our Hazard Perception Test platform provides registered schools with access-code-based entry and an interactive assessment designed for student practice and evaluation. Schools can introduce learners to realistic hazard scenarios and encourage consistent road-safety awareness as part of their training programme. Contact us to learn how your driving school can get started with the Kerala hazard perception training platform.
