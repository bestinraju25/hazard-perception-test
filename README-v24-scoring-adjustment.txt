RAJU HPT v49 – Gentler Reaction-Time Scoring

Base: v48

The HPT reaction-time scoring curve has been adjusted to be more candidate-friendly while preserving the principle that earlier hazard recognition earns more marks.

For each official hazard, within the existing 5-second response window:
- <= 1.0 second: 100% of hazard marks
- >1.0 to 2.0 seconds: 90%
- >2.0 to 3.0 seconds: 80%
- >3.0 to 4.0 seconds: 70%
- >4.0 to 5.0 seconds: 60%

Examples:
- One-hazard clip (10 marks): 10 / 9 / 8 / 7 / 6
- Two-hazard clip (5 marks each): 5 / 4.5 / 4 / 3.5 / 3 before rounding to whole marks.

This keeps timing important but avoids the previous steep drop where a correct response around 3–4 seconds could receive only 1/5 or 1/10.

No changes were made to video quality, TV compatibility, certificate behavior, report structure, or the v48 result-saving fix.
