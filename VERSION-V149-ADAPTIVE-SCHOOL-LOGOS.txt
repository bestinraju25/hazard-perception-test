HPT V149 — ADAPTIVE SCHOOL LOGO SIZING

Based on HPT V148 Complete.

The school welcome splash now measures each uploaded logo's natural aspect ratio and sizes its transparent display area to match. Wide wordmarks use a wider display area, square logos retain a square display area, and portrait logos retain their natural proportions. The logo artwork is not stretched, cropped, recoloured, or given a background.

The existing V145 welcome composition, V146 minimum 6.5-second display, V147 transparent frame, V143 desktop/mobile road backgrounds, V148 driving-school information page, and existing app features are preserved.
